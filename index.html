FAQ

Good questions.

Is BlackMessage free?

Yes. Both downloads are free and there are no ads, accounts or subscriptions.

Do my messages go through a server?

No. BlackMessage is local-first: accounts, conversations and attachments are stored in the app on your device. Nothing is uploaded to the hosting provider. That also means messages do not sync between your phone and your PC.

Which devices are supported?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) with Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome installed, and Android 8.0 and newer.

How do I install the APK?

Download BlackMessage.apk, open it from your Downloads, and allow installs from this source when Android asks. If the file is blocked, open Settings → Apps → Special access → Install unknown apps and allow your browser or file manager.

Why does Windows warn about the installer?

The setup is self-signed rather than purchased from a certificate authority. Click More info, then Run anyway. The SHA-256 hash on this page lets you verify the file you downloaded.

Can I send big files?

Yes. Files are stored uncompressed, so the limit is your device storage rather than an upload quota. Voice messages, photos, videos and documents all work in a chat.
