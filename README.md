## ⚡ Overview

**BLACKMESSAGE** is a sleek, obsidian-dark messenger built for speed and privacy across **Android** and **Windows**[cite: 4, 5]. Designed with a high-contrast dark interface (`#030305`) and neon crimson accents (`#ff3b1f`), it keeps your conversations fast, clean, and easy on the eyes day or night[cite: 4, 5].

## 📖 Tutorial: How It Works

<div align="center">
  <img src="assets/tutorial.png" alt="BLACKMESSAGE 3-Step Tutorial" width="100%" />
</div>

### Step 1 — Download the App
Choose the installer for your device:
* **Android:** Download `BlackMessage.apk` (Android 8.0 and newer)[cite: 4, 5].
* **Windows:** Download `BlackMessage-Setup.exe` (Windows 10 and 11)[cite: 4, 5].

### Step 2 — Install & Create Your Account
* **On Android:** Open the downloaded `.apk` file and allow **"Install from this source"** when prompted by Android[cite: 4, 5].
* **On Windows:** Run `BlackMessage-Setup.exe` and follow the quick setup wizard[cite: 4, 5].
* **Sign In:** Create your account with your display name and password, or log in instantly by scanning a **QR code**[cite: 4, 5].

### Step 3 — Start Chatting
* Add your friends or create a group/channel[cite: 4, 5].
* Send your first message or share photos, videos, and documents right in the chat[cite: 4, 5].
